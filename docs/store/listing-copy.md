## Full description

Flashhh is a local-first study engine for exams, interviews, and serious revision.

Feed it a PDF or paste your notes. Flashhh drafts flashcards for you to edit and approve — then schedules spaced review (SM-2), timed quizzes, and insights that show what still needs work.

Why not just use a chat app?

- Persistent decks, tags, and history on your device
- Spaced repetition that owns the loop, not a one-off quiz thread
- AI drafts only — you approve every card before it lands in a deck
- Offline by default; generation is the only cloud-dependent path
- Privacy-respecting: PDF bytes are temporary processing data, never the product database

Study modes include classic flip grading, cram before an exam, cloze deletions, reverse cards, and typed answers. Quizzes stay separate from spaced review so exam practice does not distort your schedule.
